# Kirito character animation

Character: Kirito from Sword Art Online.

Fan model: すがき（すがきれもん）, @kadu311.
Original release: https://bowlroll.net/file/96677 (14 March 2016).

The animation consists of offline raster renders of the rigged 3D model, with
procedurally posed running motion. Eight frames face right; eight face left.
The rendered frames are 256 × 320 pixels, arranged in two rows.

The author's model terms prohibit redistribution of the original model and
require contacting the author before redistribution of modified model data.
Only flattened PNG renders are included here; no model, mesh, rig, or source
texture is distributed. The character and model retain their respective rights
and are excluded from the website code's licence.
